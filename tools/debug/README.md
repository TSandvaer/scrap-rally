# tools/debug

Instruments kept for reuse: one line each. Run them from the repo root.

- `jitter-trace.ts` — counts one-pixel back-and-forth steps ("reversals") of cars, shadows and the view's scroll while cars drive steadily, with no browser; compares git refs side by side: `npx tsx tools/debug/jitter-trace.ts --ref main` (`--detail`, `--show "<signal>"`, `--frames n`).
- `bake-alloc.ts` — which functions of the world's bake path allocate, in bytes per frame once warm (V8's sampling heap profiler on a lap at 430 u/s, no browser); compares git refs side by side: `npx tsx tools/debug/bake-alloc.ts --ref main` (`--marks` = with track marks being made, `--renderer` = the whole Renderer.frame of a pack with two cars sliding and mine blasts, `--no-inline` = with the compilers' inlining off, `--pin`, `--track <id>`, `--hypot` = Math.hypot against the sum world.ts spells out).
- `marks-check.ts` — do track marks (scorches, tyre marks) keep their promises: stamped one by one = put back on a tile baked again, the canvases show it, a mark bakes nothing, only darker palette colours, nothing on void, pit floor or barrier, the ring of 128, clearing, a blast at the place of one in the race before, blasts of the race before, another track; no browser, exit code 1 if a check fails: `npx tsx tools/debug/marks-check.ts`.
- `marks-stress.ts` — what marking the track costs a frame at its worst, in headless Chrome: a firefight with 20 mine blasts in 10 s and three cars sliding, the marks switched off and on in turn, on a CPU and a GPU canvas; a git ref beside it: `npx tsx tools/debug/marks-stress.ts --ref main` (`--tracks`, `--canvas soft,gpu`, `--reps`, `--shot file.png`). Its page is `marks-stress/`.
- `skid-lag.ts` — does the own car leave the same tyre marks on a slow link as on a fast one, and the same as another player's screen shows for it: the real server and client netcode with a Renderer on a canvas that draws nothing, one scripted drive (flat out, a flick of the wheel every 1100 units, a spin-out as a mine's done to the server's car every 1800) at lag 0 and at lag 150 ± 40, a second player without lag watching; counts the stamps of the own view, those of the onlooker's view of the same car, and the own ones with no slide of the predicted car under them; a git ref beside it: `npx tsx tools/debug/skid-lag.ts --ref main` (`--seconds`, `--runs`, `--lag`, `--jitter`, `--hits 0`, `--flicks 0`, `--track`, `--detail` = a line per stamp).
- `mines-blink.ts` — does drawing mines that blink out allocate, and what do 40 mines in the view cost a frame against 6 (MINES-1): Renderer.frame() on a standing view with a pack of six and 6 mines, 40 mines, or 40 of which 20 blink out. Without a flag: V8's sampling heap profiler in Node, bytes per frame charged to client/ functions, and the mines drawn a frame. `--time`: the frame time in headless Chrome ("soft" and "gpu" canvas), the three scenes taking turns in one page load. A git ref beside it: `npx tsx tools/debug/mines-blink.ts --ref main` (`--time`, `--track`, `--canvas`, `--reps`, `--shot file.png --scene 2 --at 14`). Its page and scenes are `mines-blink/`.
- `looks-sheet.ts` — the cars' three looks (sport, racer, truck) on paper, made in Node from `client/gfx/sprites.ts`: a contact sheet as a PNG (every look in the six colours, whole, battered and burnt, on tarmac and dirt at 4x; 16 headings a look at 2x; the three side by side at 2x and 1x), one look large (`--zoom racer`), and `--check`: every look inside the sport's box, no pixel on a cell's border, the body tone the server's colour and the most common pixel of each sheet, and with `--ref <ref>` the sport's sheets against that ref's, bit for bit: `npx tsx tools/debug/looks-sheet.ts --check --ref main` (exit code 1 if a check fails; `--out file.png`).
- `fx-sheet.ts` — the catch-up visuals (M4-FX: lightning cells, boost flames, slipstream streaks, the HUD's DRAFT and BOOST cues) on paper: a contact sheet made of frames the game's own Renderer drew, in headless Chrome (cells on the ground, floating, taken, about to be back; the flame on the three looks; the streaks at three strengths; the HUD panel's states; one whole frame); `--sprites` = the catch-up sprite sheet large, made in Node (the cell pictures on five grounds, the flame's frames behind each look); `--check` = every pixel a palette colour or the cast shadow, no picture cut off at its border, the sheet's bytes and the sprites' cap with the tile pool and the HUD's canvases against the all-in cap of 16 MB (`canvas-cap.ts`; exit code 1 if a check fails); `--time` = what the visuals cost a frame in headless Chrome (a pack of six with the fields filled and absent in turn, Renderer.frame() with a 1x1 read-back, "soft" and "gpu" canvas; `--reps`, `--canvas`): `npx tsx tools/debug/fx-sheet.ts` (`--out file.png`). Its page is `fx-sheet/`.
- `fx-alloc.ts` — the catch-up visuals, rule 1 and the memory cap, no browser: Renderer.frame of a pack of six with the race HUD, the catch-up fields filled (a slipstream that swells and fades on every car, a boost burnt down on three, one of them hopping, 12 lightning cells taken and coming back, the HUD's two cues) and absent; V8's sampling heap profiler, bytes per frame charged to client/ functions; the drawImage and fillRect calls a frame; the canvases made while measuring; every canvas made and its bytes, and the sprites' cap with the tile pool and the HUD's canvases against the all-in cap of 16 MB (`canvas-cap.ts`; exit code 1 if over). A git ref beside it: `npx tsx tools/debug/fx-alloc.ts --ref main` (`--on`, `--off`, `--cars n`, `--cells n`, `--track`, `--warm`, `--frames`).
- `looks-alloc.ts` — the looks, rule 1 and the memory cap, no browser: Renderer.frame of six cars of three looks whose states keep changing (whole, battered, wreck: a sheet is painted again at every change), one hopping, one falling; V8's sampling heap profiler, bytes per frame charged to client/ functions; the sheets painted against the state changes (one each); the sprite canvases made and their bytes against `SPRITE_BYTES_MAX` and, with the tile pool and the HUD's canvases, against the all-in cap of 16 MB (`canvas-cap.ts`; exit code 1 if over). A git ref beside it: `npx tsx tools/debug/looks-alloc.ts --ref main` (`--plain` = no hop, fall or ghost, `--every n`, `--cars n`, `--track`, `--warm`, `--frames`).
- `looks-repaint.ts` — what a car's change of state costs the frame it happens in, in headless Chrome (a car sheet holds the car's state, so a car that becomes battered or a wreck mid-race has its sheet of 128 headings painted again): six cars of three looks in a standing view, one changing state every 30 frames; Renderer.frame() with a 1x1 read-back for the frames with a change and without, the repaint by itself and the first blit from the sheet, whether the repainted sheets hold the pixels they should, the sprite bytes against the cap; "soft" (CPU raster) and "gpu" canvas: `npx tsx tools/debug/looks-repaint.ts --tracks rust-bowl,crusher-run,overpass-knot` (`--canvas`, `--reps`, `--every`, `--shot file.png --at 900`; exit code 1 on a wrong pixel or bytes over the cap). Its page is `looks-repaint/`.
- `draft-tune.ts` — how long a pass by slipstream takes (M4-DRAFT): two cars on a dead-straight test track stepped through the shared stepRaceCar with the server's drafts (draftsFor), the follower `--gap` behind at `--v0` u/s, in the wake until `--pull` behind then out to lane `--lat` (or on lane `--lat` all the way); prints the time until its centre is ahead, its best lead and the time to a whole car length, for a sweep of every slipstream constant (shipped ones marked *) and of start gaps; no browser: `npx tsx tools/debug/draft-tune.ts` (`--car racer|truck`).
- `draft-race.ts` — what the catch-up does to a real race (M4-DRAFT/M4-CELLS): a Room on the sim clock with server bots (optionally the host driven by the autopilot, `--pilot`, `--pilot-levels e,t,w`), each race twice, BEFORE (no slipstream, bots hold their lanes) and AFTER; lead changes per lap (a car length clear), gaps to the autopilot, time drafting, the bots' tucks and passes, car-car contacts and crashes (closing >= 70 u/s), wall hits (those within 2 s of a mine spin counted apart), wrecks and falls: `npx tsx tools/debug/draft-race.ts --track rust-bowl --bots normal,normal --laps 3` (`--all` = every shipped track, one line each; `--noweapons`; `--cars sport,racer`).
- `catch-alloc.ts` — the catch-up mapping of client/main.ts (its "catch-up cues" block: CarView.draft/.boost, WorldFx.cells/.nCells, HudView.draft/.boost from the M4-DRAFT/M4-CELLS state), rule 1, no browser: the block cut out of main.ts and loaded by itself, driven by the real server and client Game in a race of Normal server bots and the autopilot; V8's sampling heap profiler from a caller that is never optimised (so nothing is inlined out of sight), with a control that must be seen; bytes per frame under the block, what it showed (drafts, boosts, cells taken, blinking, in the air) and the CellView contract (exit code 1 if broken or the control is not seen): `npx tsx tools/debug/catch-alloc.ts` (`--track`, `--bots`, `--lane`, `--warm`, `--settle`, `--frames`, `--chunks`; a cold start: `--warm 0 --settle -1 --chunks 12`).
- `hard-tune.ts` — the Hard bot level (M4-HARD), no browser: `--golden` = SHA-256 over every tick's bot controls and car states (full float64 bits) and the wallets, Easy and Normal on every shipped track plus the client-bot path (sim-test holds the hashes from before botdrive.ts changed); `--pace` = a lone bot's median lap per track and car type for Normal, the flat-out ceiling (CORNER_SCALE = 3) and Hard, and the share of the gap Hard closes; `--sweep k=a,b [--sweep2 k=a,b]` = that for a sweep of the `HARD` constants; `--traffic` = 3-bot races per level and car set (falls, wall contacts, wrecks, rivet and mine hits of those used); `--vs` = 3 Hard against 3 Normal, 3 races per track, places and scrap: `npx tsx tools/debug/hard-tune.ts --pace` (`--set corner=1.05,apex=20`).
- `schema-hash.ts` — does a track-schema change leave the shipped tracks' behaviour alone (M4-SCHEMA): per shipped track, sha256 over float64 bits of today's built arrays, of the track queries and validators over a dense set of points, of three server bots' control streams and car states driven by botStep and stepRaceCar (no room), and of a 3-lap Room race of them (car states, progress, shots, mines, results, wallets); each part run twice; `--write` records them in `schema-hash.golden.json`, `--check` compares (exit code 1 if any differs): `npx tsx tools/debug/schema-hash.ts --check`.
- `canvas-census.ts` — every canvas the game makes, counted against the cap of 16 MB all in (user decision 2026-11-21; `canvas-cap.ts` holds the number for every tool): the client's own code run in Node through a session that fills every pool (every track, six cars of three looks changing state, cells, boosts, the race tick, the whole HUD with more names and centre texts than their pools hold), each canvas noted with its size and who asked for it; a table by kind (world tiles, car sheets, silhouettes, effect, catch-up and obstacle sheets, font sheets, HUD labels, name tags, centre texts, minimaps, hp bars, cue art), the total, the headroom, and whether every pool was full (exit code 1 if over the cap or a pool is not full): `npx tsx tools/debug/canvas-census.ts` (`--list`).
- `world-sheet.ts` — the road's furniture on paper (M4-WORLD: water, dirt, tyre mounds, split lanes, the conveyor belt, rolling barrels, the crusher press, the ramp truck): a contact sheet made of frames the game's own Renderer drew, in headless Chrome (every feature on asphalt, dirt and across a kerb, at 2x and at game scale; the press's cycle; a barrel's turn; three whole frames of Preview Yard), on a track of the sheet's own (`world-sheet/yard.ts`) and on Preview Yard; `--puddles` = the pools and dirt patches alone, each with its declared and its traction rectangle drawn over a copy; `--sprites` = the obstacle sheet large, made in Node; `--check` = every pixel of the obstacle sheet a palette colour or the cast shadow, no barrel picture cut off, the eight frames of a turn all different, room on the sheet for what moves on every track, and a belt's arrows staying on the belt (a sweep of 2916 belts of every width, place across the road, length and speed on a straight and in two bends, every arrow pixel put back on the track) (exit code 1 if a check fails): `npx tsx tools/debug/world-sheet.ts` (`--out file.png`).
